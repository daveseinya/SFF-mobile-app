    Team entry guide

    Make the application smoother by getting these details together first.

    01

    Coach or coordinator

    Team name, contact name, phone number and email address. An SFF membership ID is also requested for the coach.

    02

    Player list

    Register the player with SFF first, then enter their unique SFF membership ID, name, shirt number and position. The name defaults to the ID and can be edited.

    03

    Competition details

    Choose Full Field or Six aside, review the entry fee, and select the team's payment method.

    Team consent

    Read and accept the liability waiver and participating team management consent to submit the entry.
